# 👋 Hi, I'm María Bernarda Salazar Sánchez

### Ph.D. in Electronic Engineering · Bioengineer · Data Scientist · Research Professor

I am a **Research Professor at Universidad de Antioquia (UdeA)**, working at the intersection of **Data Science, Artificial Intelligence, Machine Learning, and Engineering**. I am currently the **Coordinator of the Specialization in Analytics and Data Science** at the Faculty of Engineering and a researcher at the **Intelligent Information Systems Laboratory (In2Lab)**.

My research focuses on developing computational and data-driven methods to transform data into **knowledge, decision support, and intelligent solutions**.

## 🧪 Research

### 🩺 AI for Health

Research applying machine learning and explainable AI to clinical and biomedical data, including:

- Clinical prediction
- Hospital readmission
- Medical imaging
- Radiomics
- Disease classification
- Explainable AI

### 🤟 Colombian Sign Language

Research on **machine learning, computer vision and multimodal data for Colombian Sign Language (LSC)**, including video, spatiotemporal representations, and body, hand and facial landmarks.

**LSC5 — A Multi-View Video and Spatiotemporal Landmark Dataset for Colombian Sign Language**

🔗 [LSC5 Dataset](https://figshare.com/s/1f2008e0fcd6cfd151ad)

### 📡 Wearable & Biomedical Systems

Development and analysis of multimodal sensing systems combining:

`sEMG` · `IMU` · `Pressure` · `Bioimpedance` · `Temperature`
